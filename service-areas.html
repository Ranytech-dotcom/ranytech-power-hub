SEO strategy

Target the market without keyword stuffing.

The website will target searches such as solar installation in Calabar, solar company in Cross River State, inverter installation in Akwa Ibom, battery backup solutions in Uyo and related regional searches through useful service pages, real project case studies and consistent business information.

As genuine installations are completed, we can add project pages tied to the actual town, equipment, system size and customer goal. That gives Google stronger evidence than creating dozens of thin location pages.
