Professional technical skills across power, networks, connectivity and security.

RanyTech Power Hub combines practical field support across power systems, networking, wireless internet, surveillance and low-voltage infrastructure from a Calabar base.

Integrated technical capability

One problem can involve several systems at once.

A business internet problem may actually begin with unstable power. A CCTV fault may come from a failed PoE switch or damaged cable. A Wi-Fi complaint may require access-point repositioning rather than a faster internet package. RanyTech Power Hub approaches these jobs by looking at how power, network, cabling and endpoint equipment interact.

This integrated approach is useful for homes, shops, offices and larger operational environments where several technologies depend on one another.

Networking, MikroTik and Wi-Fi

Core networking capability includes RouterOS configuration, IP addressing, routing, firewall rules, hotspot and user access control, VLAN segmentation, managed switching, failover concepts and Wi-Fi coverage planning. See our dedicated MikroTik and Wi-Fi networking and network and IT infrastructure pages.

Starlink, wireless links and internet distribution

Connectivity work includes Starlink installation support, local network integration, building-to-building wireless links, access-point deployment, internet distribution and troubleshooting of unstable radio or PoE equipment. Explore Starlink and internet distribution.

CCTV and surveillance systems

Surveillance capability includes camera positioning, blind-spot review, DVR/NVR support, recording checks, CCTV cabling, PoE infrastructure and backup-power planning for critical surveillance equipment. See CCTV installation and surveillance support.

Structured cabling and low-voltage infrastructure

Physical infrastructure work covers Ethernet cabling, PoE, cable routing, intercom-related cabling, device relocation, signal-booster support and fault tracing. These services support networks, cameras, radios and other connected equipment. See structured cabling and low-voltage systems.

Solar, inverter and battery systems

Power capability includes load assessment, solar planning, inverter sizing, battery-backup planning and integration of backup power for homes, shops and technology equipment. The goal is to match system capacity to the actual load and required runtime. Explore solar installation in Calabar and inverter and battery solutions.

Fault diagnosis, maintenance and site surveys

Technical support includes systematic troubleshooting, preventive inspection, corrective maintenance, requirements gathering, site surveys, material planning and practical incident documentation. This work is especially useful where faults repeat or where a new installation needs to be planned before equipment is purchased. See IT support and technical maintenance.

Regional project support

RanyTech Power Hub is based at Tinapa Junction, Calabar. Project enquiries can also be considered from reachable areas across Cross River and Akwa Ibom where travel, survey and project scope are practical. The website does not present fake branches; regional pages describe service coverage only. See our service areas.

How to request technical help

Send your location, the system involved, the problem or goal, and photos where useful. For new installations, include the number of rooms, devices, cameras, users or appliances involved. For faults, mention what has already been tested and whether the problem is constant or intermittent.
