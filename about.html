About RanyTech Power Hub — a Calabar power and technology company.

RanyTech Power Hub is an independent Nigerian power and technology services company based at Tinapa Junction, Calabar, Cross River State. The business combines solar, inverter and battery solutions with MikroTik networking, Wi-Fi, Starlink, CCTV, structured cabling, low-voltage systems and technical maintenance for homes and businesses.

Our purpose

Built from a real local need.

Frequent electricity challenges around the area created a clear need for reliable alternatives. RanyTech Power Hub was developed as a practical response — combining solar generation, battery storage and inverter power with convenient charging services.

The goal is to serve individuals, families and businesses with useful, dependable power and technology support while growing into a trusted regional name for energy, connectivity, surveillance and IT infrastructure solutions.

Our mission

To provide accessible and dependable power, connectivity, security and IT solutions that help people and businesses stay powered, connected, protected and productive.
