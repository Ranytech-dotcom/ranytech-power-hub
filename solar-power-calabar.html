Solar power in Calabar

Start with a load assessment, not a random system size.

A dependable solar setup should be designed around what you actually need to run. A small lighting-and-device system has very different requirements from a setup expected to support televisions, refrigerators, office equipment, networking devices or other heavier loads. We begin by identifying the appliances, their approximate power demand, how many hours of backup you need and whether daytime solar production or overnight battery storage is the bigger priority.

This approach helps avoid two common problems: buying a system that cannot carry the intended load, or overspending on equipment that is larger than necessary.

What information helps us prepare a useful quote?

Before requesting a quote, list the main appliances you want the system to power. Include items such as lights, fans, televisions, refrigerators, laptops, routers, CCTV equipment or office devices. Tell us roughly how many hours you want them to operate during an outage. Photos of an existing inverter, batteries or solar panels can also help when the request is for an upgrade rather than a new installation.

Solar, inverter and battery work together as one system.

The panels generate energy, the charge-control stage manages how that energy reaches storage, the batteries determine how long stored power is available, and the inverter must be capable of supplying the connected load. For this reason, we treat solar planning together with inverter and battery sizing rather than recommending components in isolation.
