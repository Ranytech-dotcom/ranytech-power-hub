Why this project matters

The business itself demonstrates the services it is building around.

The Tinapa Junction hub combines practical backup-power planning with a local charging service. It provides a real reference point for how solar panels, battery storage and inverter capacity work together to support everyday electricity needs.

The same planning principles apply to customer projects: identify the actual load, decide the required runtime, size the inverter correctly, match battery capacity to the goal and determine how much solar charging is needed. See our solar installation and inverter and battery pages for those services.
