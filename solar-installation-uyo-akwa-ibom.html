Solar, inverter and battery project support for Uyo and Akwa Ibom.

Regional installation and backup-power enquiries are handled from our Tinapa Junction, Calabar base, with travel confirmed according to project scope.

RanyTech Power Hub accepts enquiries from Uyo and reachable parts of Akwa Ibom for solar power, inverter systems and battery backup solutions. We do not present Uyo as a separate office; projects are scheduled from Calabar when the location, system size and installation requirements make regional support practical.

Akwa Ibom areas we can consider

Uyo and Itu are among the closest Akwa Ibom locations to our Calabar base. We can also review enquiries from Eket, Ikot Ekpene, Oron and other accessible areas when the scope supports survey, transport and installation logistics.

Planning backup power for a home or business

The right system depends on more than the number of appliances. We consider their actual power draw, how many hours they run, startup demand, the required backup duration and whether solar charging should restore the batteries during the day. For a shop or office, we can also account for routers, CCTV, computers, printers and other equipment that needs stable power.

  • Solar panel and battery-storage planning
  • Inverter sizing for homes, shops and offices
  • Backup systems for internet, CCTV and IT equipment
  • Upgrade planning for undersized or ageing installations
  • Load review before a new system is purchased

From first enquiry to installation

Send your location, appliance list, estimated daily usage and preferred backup hours on WhatsApp. We can often provide an initial direction remotely. If the project needs a physical assessment, we will confirm survey arrangements before a final installation quote. This helps avoid selecting equipment based only on advertised inverter size or battery capacity.

Related services for Akwa Ibom projects

Where a project includes technology infrastructure, RanyTech can also support Starlink and internet distribution, MikroTik and Wi-Fi networking, CCTV surveillance and technical maintenance. Combining the power and network requirements at planning stage can reduce later rewiring and capacity problems.
