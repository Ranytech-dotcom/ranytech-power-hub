CCTV & surveillance

Good CCTV starts with coverage planning.

Camera quantity alone does not determine whether a surveillance system is useful. Placement, viewing angle, lighting, recording quality and the areas that matter most all need to be considered. We help assess entrances, corridors, work areas, outdoor spaces, cash points, equipment zones and other locations where visibility is important.

For an existing installation, the same review can identify blind spots, failed cameras, poor positioning, damaged cabling or recorder channels that are no longer working as expected.

CCTV services we support

  • Camera installation, relocation and positioning
  • Blind-spot review and coverage expansion
  • DVR and NVR setup or troubleshooting
  • Analog, IP and PoE camera infrastructure
  • Video/data cabling and power distribution
  • Storage and recording checks
  • Replacement of failed cameras or supporting components
  • Backup-power planning for recorders and network devices

Recorder, storage and network planning matter too.

The recorder must have enough usable channels for the current camera count and future expansion. Storage capacity also affects how long recorded footage can be retained. Where IP cameras or remote viewing are involved, the CCTV system may depend on the same switches, router, Wi-Fi or internet infrastructure used elsewhere on the site.

That is why CCTV work often connects directly with our network and IT infrastructure, MikroTik and Wi-Fi and structured cabling and low-voltage services.

Keeping cameras running during outages.

If continuous recording is important, power to the recorder, switches, PoE equipment and cameras needs to be considered. We can review the surveillance load together with inverter and battery backup planning so critical equipment does not shut down immediately when utility power fails.

New installation or upgrade?

Send the location, approximate number of cameras required and the areas you want covered. For upgrades, photos of the existing recorder, cameras and cabling can help identify the right next step before a site visit.
