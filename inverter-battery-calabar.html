Inverter and battery backup solutions in Calabar.

RanyTech Power Hub helps size backup systems around actual load, required runtime, battery capacity and charging options.

Backup power planning

Choose capacity based on the devices you need to keep running.

The inverter nameplate alone does not tell you how long a backup system will last. Runtime depends on the connected load, battery capacity, battery condition, system efficiency and how the batteries are recharged. We start by identifying the equipment that must stay on during an outage and the minimum backup time you expect.

This helps separate essential loads from optional loads and reduces the risk of buying an inverter that is powerful enough but paired with too little battery storage.

Typical backup-power requirements

  • Lighting, fans and televisions for home use
  • Routers, switches and access points that need uninterrupted connectivity
  • Office laptops, desktops, printers and communications equipment
  • CCTV cameras, DVR/NVR equipment and security networking
  • Shop equipment and selected appliances within the designed load
  • Battery-bank expansion when existing runtime has become inadequate

Battery runtime should be planned realistically.

Two customers using the same inverter can get very different backup times because their loads and battery banks are different. We use the appliance list and desired runtime to estimate the storage requirement before recommending a practical configuration. Where an existing system is involved, battery age, charging behaviour, cable condition and inverter settings also matter.

Solar charging can reduce dependence on grid power.

An inverter and battery system can be integrated with solar where the equipment and charging design support it. If solar is part of your plan, see our solar installation in Calabar page for panel, battery and load-planning guidance.

Backup power for IT, CCTV and internet equipment.

Network and security equipment often needs stable power because repeated shutdowns can interrupt internet access, camera recording and local services. We can review backup requirements alongside network infrastructure, MikroTik and Wi-Fi, Starlink or CCTV installations.

What to send before requesting a quote

Send your location, appliance list, desired backup time and whether you already own an inverter, batteries or solar panels. If you have existing equipment, include clear photos of the model labels. This makes the first assessment more useful before a site survey is arranged.
