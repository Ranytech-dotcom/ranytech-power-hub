RanyTech Power Hub solar and backup power projects across Cross River.

Regional project support from a Calabar-based power business.

RanyTech Power Hub is based at Tinapa Junction, Calabar and accepts solar, inverter and battery project enquiries from customers across reachable parts of Cross River State.

Areas we can assess

Calabar remains our base. Enquiries are also welcome from Odukpani, Akamkpa, Ikom, Ogoja and other reachable Cross River communities, subject to project size, survey needs and travel logistics.

What we help with

  • Solar power system planning for homes and shops
  • Inverter and battery backup sizing
  • Solar charging integration
  • Expansion of existing backup systems
  • Load assessment and practical power consultation

How regional projects work

Send your town, the appliances you want to power and your desired backup time on WhatsApp. We will confirm whether a remote estimate is enough or whether a site survey is required.
