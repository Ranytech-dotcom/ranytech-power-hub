Solar, inverter and battery project support across Cross River State.

RanyTech Power Hub works from Tinapa Junction, Calabar and handles regional power enquiries where project size, survey requirements and travel are practical.

Homes, shops, offices and small businesses across Cross River often need backup power that is sized around actual daily loads rather than guesswork. RanyTech Power Hub helps customers plan solar panels, inverter capacity, battery storage and backup runtime from a Calabar base, with regional support arranged according to location and project scope.

Cross River locations we can assess

Calabar Municipal, Calabar South, Akpabuyo and Odukpani are closest to our base. We also accept enquiries from Akamkpa, Ikom, Ogoja and other reachable communities when the installation or upgrade justifies travel. These are service areas, not separate RanyTech branches.

What a proper solar quote should consider

A useful recommendation starts with what you actually want to power. We review appliance wattage, expected daily use, starting loads from equipment such as refrigerators or pumps, required backup hours, available roof or ground space and whether the system should support future expansion.

  • Solar panel and charge-controller planning
  • Inverter capacity and battery-bank sizing
  • Home, shop and office backup-power systems
  • Expansion or correction of an existing solar/inverter setup
  • Load assessment for routers, CCTV, computers and other technology equipment

How regional projects are handled

Start by sending your town, appliance list and desired backup time on WhatsApp. For straightforward requirements, we can prepare an initial recommendation remotely. Larger or more complex systems may require a site survey to check installation space, cable routes, existing electrical distribution and safety requirements before a final quotation.
