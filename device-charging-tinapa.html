What can be charged?

The service is intended for everyday rechargeable devices such as phones, power banks, laptops, rechargeable fans and lamps. If you have another type of device, send a photo or the charger specification on WhatsApp first so we can confirm whether it is suitable for the available charging setup.

Why confirm availability first?

Charging time depends on device battery size, charger type and how many devices are already being handled. Calling or messaging first helps confirm that charging space is available and gives you the current rate before you travel to Tinapa Junction.

More than a charging point.

RanyTech Power Hub also works on solar power, inverter and battery backup, network infrastructure, Starlink and internet distribution and CCTV systems. The charging service is part of the wider power-and-technology focus of the business.
